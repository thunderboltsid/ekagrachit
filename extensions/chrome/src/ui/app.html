Ekagrachit

Ekagrachit lives in your menu bar.

Click ◎ at the top of your screen to choose what to focus on: this page, or any window on your Mac. This extension does its part in Chrome, keeping links away from your page from loading.

Don't see ◎? Open Ekagrachit from your Applications folder, or download it.

In a focus session? Open Ekagrachit: it picks the session up where it was. To end it early, press ⌃⌥⌘E and hold.
